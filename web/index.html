How to read this

The map follows the results plates: each 100 m square is people in 2020 minus 1990 (GHS-POP), green rings are the central / intermediate / distant zone edges, brown lines are LGAs, and the yellow star is the founding centre. φ is how far the typical resident moved versus the national fit.

Glossary
Remoteness r
1000 · distance (km) / √(2020 population). Central under 3, intermediate 3–5, distant 5–9.3, peri-urban beyond.
Central share
Share of the city’s people living in the central zone.
φ (spread factor)
How much farther from the centre the typical resident sat in 2020 versus 1990. Below the national fit means the city spread less than expected for its growth.
